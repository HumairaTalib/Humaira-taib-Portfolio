🍗Chicken Biryani

Healthy . Everyon's favourite dish!

Ingrediants:
  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1 kg boneless chicken
  • 2 tablespoon oil
  • 1 🥄 tablespoon cumin seeds
Instructions:
  • Heat oil in a pan and saute` cumin seeds
  • Add chicken and cook until browned.
  • Add rice & water to the pan and bring to a boil.
  • Reduce heat and simmer until rice is cooked.
  • Your biryani is ready to eat😊
🥣Creamy Mac & Veggie Pasta

A tasty combinatin of vegetables and macrony.

Ingrediants:
  • 1 cup small pasta (elbows or shells)
  • 1/2 cup steamed carrots (mashed)
  • 1/2 cup steamed broccoli (chopped small)
  • 2 tbsp milk
Instructions:
  • Cook pasta and drain.
  • Mix in mashed veggies, cheese, and milk
  • Stir on low heat until creamy
  • Add more milk for smoother texture for toddlers.
  • Your Yummy Pasta is ready to eat😊
🥪Turkey & Avocado Roll-Ups

No crust • Soft & protein-rich

Ingrediants:
  • 2 slices whole wheat bread (crust removed)
  • 2 slices turkey
  • 1/4 avocado, mashed
Instructions:
  • Flatten bread slices with a rolling pin.
  • Spread avocado, add turkey, roll up, and slice into pinwheels.
  • ✅ Great for lunchboxes and picky eaters.

Warm • Easy to swallow • Comforting
